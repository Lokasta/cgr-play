Credit and user-declared specific project-use authorization are recorded in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md); no public license or independently inspected legal contract is asserted.

The [decoder report](../../../../docs/qa/derringer-integration-20261022/decoder/REPORT.md) and [historical decoder recipe](../../../../docs/qa/derringer-integration-20261022/decoder/reproduce.py) retain that evidence. This intake did not repeat extraction or open an original game/cache bundle; those bundles are not included.
2. **Authored ABF ancestor.** The retained [Blender source](../../../../art/weapons/remington_derringer/cleaning-uv-source.blend), [unwrap input](../../../../art/weapons/remington_derringer/unwrap-input.json) and [UV snapshot](../../../../art/weapons/remington_derringer/cleaning-uv.json) document the authored UV stage. [runtime-uv-manifest.json](../../../../art/weapons/remington_derringer/runtime-uv-manifest.json) describes the ancestor [runtime-ancestor-18cd.glb](../../../../art/weapons/remington_derringer/runtime-ancestor-18cd.glb), SHA-256 `18cd9211715230002d65996c136981b82721f3d5914738865663ec8c19323f76`.
3. **Accepted focal gutter repair.** The immutable [repair manifest](../../../../art/weapons/remington_derringer/gutter-fix-manifest.json), SHA-256 `412c403d009506c3bed7edf757731761f1a79eae66a69e946bbdf653cb20d036`, records the ancestor18cd → runtime16cf transformation. [RECIPE.md](../../../../art/weapons/remington_derringer/RECIPE.md) provides the portable CPU replay and exact local input hashes. It reconstructs final16cf from retained ancestor18cd without extraction, Blender, re-unwrap or material changes.

The immutable [canonical snapshot](../../../../art/weapons/remington_derringer/canonical-parts.ancestor.json), SHA-256 `52aeacb4ba4043a8a2138e828776988add865a59b9a12037e52ec876f467220f`, declares **ancestor18cd**, even though a copy existed beside final16cf. Its asset hash must not be interpreted as the final runtime hash. The new [consumer manifest](runtime-manifest.json) binds final16cf explicitly and preserves its 23 canonical parts, their 28 owned mesh nodes, plus fixed frame = 29 meshes, 31 nodes, 37 primitives and 9582 triangles. `_pCube1` is a non-renderable transform helper. The [F6 closure](../../../../docs/qa/derringer-integration-20261022/review/f6-closure.md) and [root acceptance](../../../../docs/qa/derringer-integration-20261022/coordination/asset-intake-acceptance.json) record the existing acceptance; this intake introduces no new geometry review.

UV0 is the cleaning atlas and UV1 is the source appearance mapping. The accepted gutter repair is specific to **1024** masks for this Derringer. Legacy glTF materials have metallic 0 / roughness 0.6 and approximate the original shader; the runtime author owns Cozy finish classification. [audio-materials.json](audio-materials.json) preserves pearl grip bindings. Source images, geometry, materials and animation bytes were not changed by intake. This provenance/replay check does not prove gameplay coverage, appearance approval, performance or publication.

All `sources` keys in runtime-manifest.json are repository-relative file paths **resolved from that manifest's directory**, with hashes computed from actual local bytes. Art files are retained through the project's DVC workflow and are not web downloads; public metadata lists portable repository paths only. Historical art snapshots retain original forensic paths as immutable evidence; the portable wrapper resolves inputs adjacent to itself and never follows those historical absolute paths.
