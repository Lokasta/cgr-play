# Cozy Gun Restoration — Media Kit

## Official name

Cozy Gun Restoration

## One-line description

A cozy, tactile game about carefully cleaning and restoring old revolvers at home.

## Short description

Cozy Gun Restoration is a quiet, hands-on restoration game. Receive worn revolvers, clean and
care for each part, reassemble the mechanism, and display the finished piece in a late-night home
workshop. The experience is about patience, craft, and atmosphere rather than combat.

## Logo usage

- Use the white logo on black or very dark backgrounds.
- Use the black logo on white or very light backgrounds.
- Prefer the full wordmark at 320 px wide or larger; use the compact mark below that size.
- Preserve the supplied proportions and clear space.
- Keep the identity monochrome. Do not add gradients, outlines, shadows, glow, texture, or bevels.
- Do not recolor individual letters, rotate the mark, or separate the sparkle from the cylinder.
